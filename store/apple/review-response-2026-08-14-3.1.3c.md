# App Review response — Guidelines 3.1.1 / 3.1.3(c)

Submission: `8e6e25c3-30ff-41b4-a4a5-4469b596800e`  
App: A+ SmartDocs  
Review date: 14 August 2026

## Positioning

A+ SmartDocs is an enterprise service sold directly by A+ Solution GmbH to companies and organizations for use by their employees and authorized team members.

A+ SmartDocs is not sold to consumers, individual users, or families. Individual employees do not purchase access themselves. Their organization receives a company account and provides access to its staff.

The iOS app is used to sign in to an existing organization account and use the document workflow already provisioned for that organization.

## Changes made after review

- Removed all wording that offered the service to individual users.
- Removed the free-trial call to action from the iOS login experience.
- Registration is now explicitly an enterprise access request made on behalf of a company or organization and is not a purchase or subscription checkout.
- Removed plan-change and upgrade actions from the authenticated billing area.
- The account area now states that no plans, subscriptions, upgrades, or digital add-ons can be purchased or changed inside the mobile app.
- Terms now explicitly state that the service is not sold to consumers, individual users, or families.
- Added regression tests to prevent consumer-sales wording and in-app upgrade actions from returning.
